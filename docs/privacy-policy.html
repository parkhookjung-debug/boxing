Privacy Policy — Boxing Game

Last updated: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Boxing Game application ("the App", package name com.parkhookjung.boxinggame) handles your information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect or store your personal data on our servers. We do not operate any backend that receives your information. However, the App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, and the AdMob SDK may automatically collect certain information for advertising purposes (see Section 3).

2. Camera Use

The App requests the Camera permission solely to detect your body pose and recognize boxing punches in real time. The camera feed is processed entirely on your device using an on-device machine-learning model (MediaPipe Pose Landmarker). Camera images and pose data:

  • are processed only in memory while you use the App;
  • are never saved, recorded, uploaded, or shared;
  • are discarded immediately after each frame is processed.

The camera is never used for advertising and is never transmitted off the device.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK and Google may collect and process information such as your advertising identifier (Advertising ID), IP address, device information, and app interaction data. This data is handled by Google as a third party.

  • Google's use of advertising data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), or opt out of ad personalization.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party that receives data is Google AdMob, and only for the advertising purposes described above.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Permissions Summary

  • Camera — required for on-device pose/punch detection. Images are not stored or transmitted.
  • Internet — required only to load ads via Google AdMob.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact: parkhookjung@gmail.com
